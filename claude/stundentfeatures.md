# Student: Feature Specification

A list of everything a student can do in the platform. No technical detail here; this describes what the product does.

**Module tags** (shown in each heading) show which sellable module a feature belongs to, so a student only sees what their college has bought.

---

## 1. Account and Access `Core`
1. Receive an invite email from the placement office and activate the account.
2. Sign in with email and a one-time code, or a link.
3. Only the college's own page and data are visible; a student cannot see other colleges.
4. Sign out of all devices.
5. Install the app on the phone or desktop home screen (PWA).
6. See a clear message if the account is blocked, deactivated, or the college's module is off.

---

## 2. Dashboard `Core`
1. Profile completion progress, with the next section to fill.
2. Verification status: what is verified, what is waiting, what needs changes.
3. Open drives I am eligible for, with deadlines.
4. My applications and their current round.
5. Upcoming rounds and interviews in the next 7 days.
6. Latest announcements from the placement office.
7. Notifications that need action: changes requested, offer awaiting response, round starting soon.
8. Active blocks and why, if any.

---

## 3. My Profile and Onboarding `Onboarding module`

### 3.1 Fill the profile
Sections, each with its own status (draft, submitted, changes requested, verified):
1. Personal details
2. Academics: 10th, 12th, semester-wise marks, backlogs
3. Education history
4. Skills
5. Projects
6. Internships
7. Resume
8. Supporting documents: marksheets, ID proof

Features across all sections:
1. Save a draft and continue later.
2. Upload documents and resumes, with a preview and replace option.
3. Submit a section for review.
4. See the officer's comment when changes are requested, and edit only what is flagged.
5. Resubmit after changes.
6. Verified sections are locked; to change one, send a correction request with the reason.
7. See who verified a section and when.
8. Get a clear checklist of what is required before verification is complete.

### 3.2 Personality and aptitude assessment `Psychometrics module`
1. See why the test is asked and how the results will be used before starting.
2. Give or withdraw consent; the test cannot start without consent.
3. Take the test in parts, and resume if interrupted.
4. See my own results: strengths, work style, suggested role areas, with a plain explanation.
5. Retake after a set period.
6. Results are never used to decide eligibility or block me from a drive.
7. Choose whether to share results with the placement office at individual level.
8. Request deletion of my results.

### 3.3 Placement participation status `Onboarding module`
1. See my current status: registered, opted out, higher studies, entrepreneurship, or not eligible.
2. Request a status change with a reason; the officer approves or rejects it.
3. See the history of my status.

### 3.4 My full view `Onboarding module`
1. One page with my profile, marks, documents, drives, rounds, offers, and blocks.
2. Download my profile as a PDF.

---

## 4. Drives `Drives module`

### 4.1 Browse drives
1. List of drives: open, closing soon, upcoming, past.
2. Filter by company, role, job type, package, location, and tier.
3. Drive page: company, role, description, package, location, venue, rounds and dates, attached documents.
4. See the eligibility criteria.
5. If I am not eligible, see exactly which criteria I miss (for example "CGPA below 7.0" or "profile not verified").
6. See the application deadline with a countdown.
7. Save a drive to review later.

### 4.2 Apply
1. Apply with one tap if eligible; the resume used is saved as it was at the time.
2. Choose which uploaded resume to apply with, where the college allows it.
3. Withdraw an application before the deadline.
4. Clear errors if I am blocked, past the deadline, or not eligible; the system, not the screen, enforces this.

### 4.3 Track my applications
1. List of all my applications and their status.
2. Round-by-round timeline for each drive: applied, attended, cleared, rejected, with dates.
3. See my score and remarks per round, where the college shares them.
4. See which round I stopped at, and a summary after each drive.
5. See next steps: next round date, venue, and instructions.

### 4.4 Offers
1. See offers: company, role, package, offer letter.
2. Accept or decline an offer within the allowed time, with a confirmation step.
3. See college rules that apply, such as one offer per student.
4. Upload joining confirmation and date, where the college tracks joining.
5. See my placement status update after accepting.

### 4.5 Job alerts
1. Openings shared by the placement office outside formal drives, matched to my department, batch, or skills.
2. Mark interested, and open the external link.

### 4.6 Calendar
1. One calendar of all my drive dates, rounds, tests, and deadlines.
2. Reminders before a round.
3. Add an event to my phone calendar.

---

## 5. Drive Day: Attendance `Attendance & Blocking module`
1. Check in to a drive or round by scanning a QR code or entering a code.
2. See that my attendance was recorded, with the time.
3. See my attendance history: attended and missed drives.
4. Be warned if missing drives could lead to a block.
5. See the current round status and what is next.

---

## 6. Blocking `Attendance & Blocking module`
1. See if I am blocked: reason, start date, end date.
2. See the college's blocking rules in plain language.
3. Be notified when a block starts, changes, or ends.
4. Raise a request to review a block, with a reason.

---

## 7. Preparation `Student preparation module`
1. AI mock interviews by role and topic, with feedback and a history of past attempts.
2. Practice resources and guides set by the placement office.
3. Read interview experiences shared by seniors, after moderation.
4. Book counselling or mentoring sessions.
5. See my readiness summary: profile completeness, assessment scores, drives attended.

---

## 8. Resume Tools `Talent showcase module`
1. Resume feedback: completeness, keywords, and an improvement checklist.
2. Generate a resume in the college's format from my verified data.
3. Keep several resume versions and choose which to use per application.
4. Shareable profile link for employers, which I control and can switch off.

---

## 9. Assessments `Assessments module`
1. See the tests assigned to me, with dates and duration.
2. Take a test in the app with a timer and auto-save.
3. Resume after a short disconnection.
4. See results and scores after the test, where the college shares them.
5. Take a baseline diagnostic test and see where I stand against my cohort.
6. Practice tests with explanations.

---

## 10. Learning and Events `Events & Learning modules`
1. Browse events: career fairs, hackathons, coding contests, workshops.
2. Register, and see event details and my registration status.
3. See event results and certificates.
4. Join learning programmes assigned to me, and track progress.

---

## 11. Notifications and Announcements `Core`
1. In-app notifications for every status change, request, and reminder.
2. Email notifications for important events.
3. Announcements from the placement office, with an archive.
4. Mark as read, and filter by type.
5. Notification preferences: choose which emails to receive.

---

## 12. Help and Support `Core`
1. Raise a support request, for example "my marks are wrong" or "I can't apply".
2. Attach files and screenshots.
3. See the request status and the officer's replies.
4. A correction request on verified data goes straight to the verification queue.
5. FAQs and placement rules published by the college.

---

## 13. Privacy and My Data `Core`
1. See what data the college holds about me, and who can see it.
2. See and manage consent, including the personality assessment.
3. Download my data.
4. Request correction or deletion where the college allows it.
5. See a log of when staff opened my profile.

---

## 14. Rules that apply across all features
1. A student sees only their own data.
2. Eligibility, blocks, and deadlines are enforced by the system, not just hidden in the interface.
3. Verified data cannot be changed without a new review.
4. Personality data is separate from academic data and never affects eligibility.
5. Opted-out students do not appear in eligibility lists.
6. A student only sees modules the college has bought.
7. Works on mobile as an installable app, and every screen works on a small phone.

---
